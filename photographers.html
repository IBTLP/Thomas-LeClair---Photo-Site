For photographers

I’m sharing a small set of tools I’ve built for car shows — because this craft is part of what I love, and the more we raise the bar, the better it gets for everyone.

DOWNLOAD: AUTO HOBBYIST PRESETS (ZIP)
RAW-first. On JPEG, start with "Noon Sun Safe" or "Open Shade Clean," then reduce Highlights + Contrast if it feels harsh.
Fast workflow: Apply preset → adjust Exposure → pull Highlights down if paint/chrome is hot → lift Shadows a touch if wheels/grille are dark.
Install: Lightroom → Presets panel → "..." menu → Import Presets → select the ZIP (or unzip and import the folder).

01 — Outdoor — Noon Sun Safe

Best for: Harsh overhead sun (11am–3pm). The safe default.

Look: Protects highlights, opens shadows, stays clean on paint/chrome.

Quick fix: Too flat → add a touch of Contrast, or use “Noon Sun Pop.”

02 — Outdoor — Noon Sun Pop

Best for: Harsh sun when you want extra punch and presence.

Look: More contrast/depth + subtle vignette to center attention.

Quick fix: Highlights too hot → reduce Highlights/Whites; gritty → lower Clarity.

03 — Outdoor — Open Shade Clean

Best for: Overcast, shade, tents, trees, building shade.

Look: Brightens cleanly and helps counter green/yellow shade casts.

Quick fix: Still warm/green → nudge Temp cooler and Tint slightly.

04 — Outdoor — CPL Gloss Control

Best for: CPL/polarizer shots, strong reflections, blue skies.

Look: Controlled reflections + clean glossy paint feel.

Quick fix: Feels dark → lift Exposure slightly.

05 — Indoor — Mixed Light Neutral

Best for: Convention centers / mixed lighting.

Look: Cleaner color, reduced mixed-light casts, mild vignette.

Quick fix: Too warm/cool → adjust Temp; color weird → adjust Tint slightly.

06 — Indoor — High ISO Clean

Best for: ISO 3200+ or noisy indoor files, dark cars.

Look: Smoother microcontrast + stronger NR for a clean file.

Quick fix: Too soft → increase Sharpening Amount a touch.
