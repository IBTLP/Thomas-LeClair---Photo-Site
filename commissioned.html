FAQ

How do I book a commission?

Start with a short message that includes the car, your city, and what you want the images used for (legacy, builder portfolio, or sale-ready). I’ll reply with a recommended option, scope, and next steps.

Do you provide raw files?

No. Deliverables are a curated set of finished images—edited for consistency and delivered ready for print and web.

How many final images will I receive?

Enough to match the scope. Essential is a tight curated set. Signature becomes a cohesive collection. Château is outcome-driven depth scoped to the project.

What’s the turnaround time?

Typically 1–3 weeks depending on scope and season. If you have a deadline, include it in your initial request.

Do you travel?

Yes—Château Commissions are available worldwide. Travel is scoped per project.

What happens if weather doesn’t cooperate?

We adjust timing or reschedule. The goal is the images—not forcing a bad day.

Can you help with locations?

Yes. I’ll recommend locations that match the look and avoid visual clutter.

What’s included with books and prints?

Signature and Château can include a book and a fine print as part of the experience. Final size, finish, and presentation are selected together and priced to match your intent.

Owner Prep Guide

You don’t need to overthink this—but a little prep makes a big difference in the final images.

Detail matters

  • Full detail recommended (paint, wheels, glass, interior). Same-day detail is ideal.
  • Clean tires/wheels are the fastest way to make a car look expensive in photos.

Bring a quick touch-up kit

  • Window cleaner + microfiber (glass shows everything)
  • Soft duster or clean microfiber for light dust
  • Optional: light tire dressing (use sparingly—best applied after positioning)

Lights without idling

Headlights and interior lighting can add a unique look—especially during golden hour. A strong battery helps so we can keep the engine off. Idling can create subtle vibrations that reduce sharpness.

Timing makes the look

Golden hour (sunrise/sunset) is the most flattering light. Midday is workable, but harsher and less forgiving.

Weather and flexibility

If weather doesn’t cooperate, we’ll adjust timing or reschedule. The goal is the images—not forcing a bad day.
