No. Deliverables are a curated set of finished images—edited for consistency and delivered ready for print and web.

Signature and Château include a book and a fine print as part of the experience. Final size, finish, and presentation are selected together and priced to match your intent.

Owner Prep Guide

You don’t need to overthink this—but a little prep makes a big difference in the final images.

Detail matters

  • Full detail recommended (paint, wheels, glass, interior). Same-day detail is ideal.
  • Clean tires/wheels are the fastest way to make a car look expensive in photos.

Bring a quick touch-up kit

  • Window cleaner + microfiber (glass shows everything)
  • Soft duster or clean microfiber for light dust
  • Optional: a light tire dressing (use sparingly—best applied after the car is positioned)

Keep the car “photo-ready”

  • Remove parking passes, cables, clutter, and anything in cup holders
  • If we’re shooting interior, wipe screens and the gauge cluster

Lights without idling

Headlights and interior lighting can add a unique look—especially during golden hour. A strong battery helps so we can keep the engine off. Idling can create subtle vibrations that reduce sharpness—especially in close-ups and interior shots.

Timing makes the look

Golden hour (sunrise/sunset) is the most flattering light. Midday is workable, but it’s harsher and less forgiving.

Weather and flexibility

If weather doesn’t cooperate, we’ll adjust timing or reschedule—no stress.
